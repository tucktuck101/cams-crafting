# Crafting Recipes

Listed below are the Consolidated Recipes for crafting Ingots, Leather, Cloths, Wools, and Felts along with example recipes for Armor, Clothes and Capes/Mantles. For specific items, refer to Kibbles Crafting Guide.

> **Note:** For non-component items, the DC is the base DC of the item, plus the Exotic Material's DC Modifier.
>
> Also, If an item can be crafted from multiple materials e.g. Studded Leather (Metals + Leather), Halberd (Metal + Wood) the finished item can only benefit from one Exotic Material, chosen at the time of crafting.
