# Material Rarity Preface

This handout is to give an in-depth indication of the scarcity of each rarity of material. Commonly found materials, such as iron, steel, gold, or other generic forms of similar items, are excluded from this explanation.

## Uncommon

Uncommon materials are usually found in small quantities in naturally formed veins or areas where the conditions are just right. While not terribly rare, they still hold value above mundane materials.

These materials can usually be utilised by an above-average craftsman, provided they have the time and motivation. While not usually able to be purchased by common merchants, any self-respecting materials provider would at least have a decent stock of them for their most valuable customers. Larger merchant groups have no such problems dealing with this lack of inventory.

## Rare

While the uncommon materials may be a bit harder to find, rare materials are only seen in certain places or provided by certain nations in limited quantities, due to the exacting nature of processing or the difficulty of obtaining them.

While much more costly, they can still be handled to an extent by a craftsman with a lot of experience under their belt, but unless they are a specialist in their field, results may vary. Only large merchant groups have any of these materials in significant quantities and sell them for exorbitant prices.

## Very Rare

Few surviving records of these materials have made it to the present day, so only some of the most eccentric craftsmen or those who have spent a lifetime working with strange materials, such as some of the Dwarf Clans of Durgron, would be able to bring out their full potential, though in some cases, not even that is enough to bring up more than an imitation.

Merchants view these materials as lost leads and don't go out of their way to purchase them if they are found unless they have contacts in either Ravenshell or Durgron, though that doesn't stop them from holding them up as auction items for eccentric buyers.

## Legendary

Legendary materials exist almost entirely beyond the reach of ordinary craftsmen. There is no reliable mundane method of obtaining or producing them, though they may still exist within the Material Plane. They are believed to arise through one of three extraordinary circumstances: divine favour, where a deity or powerful divine being grants or manifests the material; extra-planar origin, where the material comes from another plane or exists within the realm of a powerful entity; or an ancient ritual lost to time, requiring knowledge, ingredients, magic, or circumstances that no living craftsman can reliably reproduce.

Because of this, legendary materials are treated less like resources and more like miracles. Many craftsmen would consider themselves fortunate simply to see one, while merchant groups rarely assign them a price at all. Some are known only through ancient artifacts or fragmented records, while others exist only as rumours among scholars and adventurers. A legendary material may be granted by a god, recovered from another plane, or rediscovered through a forgotten ritual—but there is no ordinary path to obtaining one.
